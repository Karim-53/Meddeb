                  Voice commands
                  
    
    
    
                  During a session, after a pause of about 1 s, say:
      «الكلمة التالية», «كلمة تالية» or
      «الكلمة الموالية» (the next word, or the lost word Meddeb waits on; before your first word, the first one only plays the ayah before, and in Blind shows the end of the page before: say it again for the word; «كلمة» alone works too, with about 1 s of silence before and
      after it),
      «باقي الآية» or «كمّل الآية» (as ⏮, the rest of the ayah),
      «عاودها مرّة», «عاودها» (no silence needed) or «عاود مرّة» (Yasser Al-Dossary plays the ayah up to where you are; before your first word, the ayah before (at a surah's start, the last ayah of the surah before and the surah's name)),
      «صدق الله العظيم» (as ■, stop and save), «استراحة قصيرة» or
      «استراحة» (as ❚❚). Some other forms of these phrases work too.
      A command is never a mistake. Off: nothing fires (the phrases are still never mistakes). Applies from the next
      start.
                  
    
    
                  Yasser Al-Dossary plays the word revealed by «الكلمة التالية» and the words
      revealed by «باقي الآية» (the microphone pauses meanwhile). The replay always plays, and
      so does the ayah before that the first «الكلمة التالية» plays before your first word; the
      ◂ / ⏮ buttons stay silent.

                  Buttons and shortcuts
                  
    
                  Buttons (right to left): ◂ next word (in a session: the page's first short red or taupe line first) · ⏮ next ayah (both pale green when a press counts as read, tan when it counts as a mistake) · microphone = start, ❚❚ pause / resume · search = find where I am (in a session: ■ stop and save). ✗ n: tap = this session's / this page's mistakes; long press = the session summary with ◀ ▶ (older / newer saved sessions) and ↺ reset. On the page: tap a word = its details; double tap a word = a new mistake, or a mistake Meddeb found in this or your last session, one just added, or a hint removed (again = undo: Meddeb's verdict back). Shortcuts: S start/stop · P pause/resume · F find where I am · N next word · A next ayah · R reset · H shown / hidden / blind · V display mode · ← next page · → previous page · ↓ end of the page before (Blind, an empty page; or pull down) · Esc close

                    Updates
                    
    
    
                    A new version downloads by itself on Wi-Fi (never on mobile data) and installs when you leave
      Meddeb, never during a recitation or while Listen plays. Ticking it asks Android once to let Meddeb install apps
      (Install unknown apps › Allow from this source). Off by default.
                    
    
    
    
                    Off: the stable versions only, tested longer: the right choice for most users. Ticked: every new
      version as soon as it is out. Going back to stable keeps the version you have until a newer stable comes.
                    
  
                    
  
                    A local and private Qur'an recitation coach: it listens while you recite from memory, follows you word by word on the
    Madani mushaf and marks the words you missed or changed. Hafs an Asim.
                    
  
                    Free, ad-free and spy-free, forever: no data collection, nothing resold.
                    
  
                    Private by design. Your voice is processed on this device and never leaves it; recordings and marks stay in
    your profile. The network is used only to download the speech model (once), the reciter's audio from
    EveryAyah.com (Listen and the voice commands; Android keeps the files on the device) and, on Android, to look for a
    new version on Meddeb's website (when About opens and after 10 minutes of use) and download it when you press
    Update (or by itself on Wi-Fi with Auto update on Wi-Fi above); nothing about you is sent.
                    
  
                    AI makes mistakes. The feedback is automatic help for your practice, not a ruling and not a replacement for a
    teacher: check any verdict by replaying your own recording, and correct it in one tap.

                    Recite
                    
  
                    Voice commands: what to say
                    
  
                    During a session, while the microphone listens, say:
                    
  
                    
    
                    «الكلمة التالية»
                    or «كلمة تالية»
                    or «الكلمة الموالية»
                    or «كلمة» alone                    		Next word: the next word appears, or the lost word Meddeb waits on, and Yasser Al-Dossary recites it (◂ first reveals the page's short red or taupe line). Say «كلمة» alone with about 1 second of silence before and after it; next to a word of the passage that sounds like it, only the full phrase works.
      Before your first word (not after Find where I am), the first one reveals and counts nothing: Yasser recites the ayah before (at a surah's start,
      the last ayah of the surah before and the surah's name) and, in Blind on an empty page, the end of the page before shows; say it again for the first word
      (it then counts as a hint).
                    
    
                    «باقي الآية»
                    or «باقي آية»
                    or «بقية الآية»
                    or «كمّل الآية»
                    or «أعطيني باقي الآية»                    		Rest of the ayah: the rest of the ayah appears (as ⏮) and Yasser recites it.
                    
    
                    «عاودها مرّة»
                    or «عاودها»
                    or «عاود مرّة»
                    or «عاودها مرّة أخرى»
                    or «عاودلي»
                    or «أعدها مرّة»
                    or «عاود», «أعدها» or «عيدها» alone                    		Replay: Yasser recites the ayah from its start up to where you are; before your first word, the ayah
      before it (at a surah's first ayah: the previous surah's last ayah, then the surah's name, a recording).
      «عاودها» and the longer phrases work with no silence around them: the surest choice. Say «عاود», «أعدها» or «عيدها» alone with about 1 second of silence before and after it; next to a word of the passage that sounds like it, only a longer phrase works.
                    
    
                    «صدق الله العظيم»
                    or «صدق الله مولانا العظيم»
                    or «صدق الله العلي العظيم»                    		Stop: the session ends and is saved (as ).
                    
    
                    «استراحة قصيرة»
                    or «استراحة»
                    or «راحة قصيرة»
                    or «استراحة صغيرة»                    		Pause: the microphone pauses (as ); press  to go on.
                    
  
                    
  
                      
    
                    • Leave about 1 second of silence before a command, then say it in one go, at your normal pace.
                      • 
    
                    • A command is never counted as a mistake; said twice in a row, it acts once. The words it reveals count as
      hints, as with the buttons.
                      • 
    
                    • While Yasser recites, the microphone waits for him; any button stops him.
                      • 
    
                    • Settings can turn the commands off, or keep Yasser silent when a command reveals words.
                      • 
    
                    • On Android the same phrases work in the car's Recite (Android Auto).

                    The buttons (right to left)
                    
  
                      
    
                    • ◂ next word and ⏮ rest of the ayah show what you forgot (during a session they count as
      mistakes, in brown; ◂ first reveals the first short red or taupe line of the page shown, a mistake on a word you
      have not said yet, if there is one; in Blind, paused or with no session, they read in your mind and count as read,
      see Text mode). The new word always
      comes on screen: its page opens and a hidden line scrolls into view. They are pale green while a press counts
      as read (no session, or read in your mind), tan while it counts as a mistake.
                      • 
    
                    •  Start a session on the page shown; you may begin anywhere on the
      page. The first press loads the speech model (on Android it listens at once meanwhile: recite, Meddeb catches up
      once the model is loaded; if it cannot load, nothing is saved). A phone downloads it by itself once (about 130 MB): a thin bar above
      the buttons shows the progress, the microphone has a dashed outline meanwhile, and a press then starts once it
      is there (press again to cancel); without internet it tries again by itself. During a session: 
      pause,  go on.
                      • 
    
                    •  Find where I am: recite from anywhere, Meddeb finds the place and
      starts the session there. During a session it is  stop and save:
      the summary lists your mistakes; tap one to open it.
                      • 
    
                    • ✗ n: this session's mistakes (tap: this page's). Long press: the session summary, with ◀ / ▶ for
      the older / newer saved sessions (each one opens on the page with its colours and your recording) and
      ↺ Reset (outside a session: clears the verdicts, the counters and the revealed words; saved sessions stay).
      ⚠: what I hear does not match the page. The small bar: green, go ahead; orange, slow down; red, wait for
      the recognition to catch up; empty while paused, hidden outside a session.
                      • 
  
                    
  
                    A session follows you into the next surah and onto the next pages; it ends only when you stop it.
                    
  
                    A message above the buttons hides by itself; its ✕ hides it at once.
                    
  
                    Colours on the words
                    
  
                      
    
                    • Red: a mistake (another word heard); underlined: skipped; dotted underline:
      lost, you jumped ahead. In Hidden and Blind, during a session, a mistake on a word you have not said correctly yet
      is a short red line: the word stays hidden until you say it, ◂ reveals it, or the session stops.
                      • 
    
                    • Taupe: being confirmed as a mistake (a taupe glow, or a short taupe line
      in Hidden and Blind); within seconds it turns red or clears.
                      • 
    
                    • Brown: revealed with ◂ / ⏮ or a voice command (a hint).
                      • 
    
                    • A soft glow behind a word is its history (past readings and this session's final verdicts): red = 2 or more
      mistakes in your last 5 readings, orange = 1, green = recovered (none in the last 5, at least one in the last
      10).
                      • 
  
                    
  
                    A tap on a word
                    
  
                    Shows what was heard, the verdict and the word's history; plays your own recording of the word or of its ayah
    (words of this session or of a saved one); + Mistake, + Correct and Remove correct the verdict,
    now or any time later. A tap on a short red or taupe line shows the same without the word itself. Closing the
    window stops your recording.
                    
  
                    Double tap a word: it becomes a mistake; a mistake Meddeb found in this session (or in your last one, still red),
    or one you just added, is marked correct instead, and a hint (◂) is taken back; double tap again to undo: Meddeb's
    own verdict comes back as it was (skipped, wrong, lost or the hint). (During a recitation, a word you have not
    recited yet only opens its window.)
                    
  
                    A red  between two words is a word you said where none is written (an insertion): tap it to
    hear it and see when it happened; double tap between two words (or + Inserted before in a word's window) to
    mark one, again to undo.
                    
  
                    The page
                    
  
                      
    
                    • Text mode (top right): Shown, Hidden (grey blocks as long as the words), Blind (nothing
      until recited or revealed; an ayah's number appears with the next ayah's first word, not as soon as the ayah

      tap a word you got wrong, the microphone button listens again. Hidden is practice: a session that was in Hidden

      PC: drag down with the mouse, or ↓) to see the last lines of the page before, greyed: as many as it takes to know
      exactly where this page starts, sometimes one or two more. A page that starts a surah says its name instead. A
      tap, a pull up, the first word you say or reveal, or a page turn puts them away.

      fit width (bigger text, the page scrolls),  own size (pinch with two
      fingers).

      last, on the phone or in the car; then Weak page, Next to learn, Manzil), Surahs and Recite
      (hands-free: Find where I am first, then Continue, Next to learn, Weak page, Manzil; the phone's microphone
      listens, a mistake clicks, the voice commands work).

                    • While a recitation plays in the car, its screen shows the last lines you recited (the next words stay hidden,
      mistakes in red) and has buttons: Next word, Rest of the ayah (Yasser says them) and Repeat the
      ayah. While listening, the screen shows the ayah being played and a Repeat button (no repeat, 1, 3, 7,
      17).
                      • 
    
                    • Listen or Recite started in the car stops and saves a recitation open on the phone, like ■.
                      •

      connects, with ▶ or with Last played), Yasser Al-Dossary recites the travel dua (Az-Zukhruf 43:13–14) and the
      ta'awwudh, then your listening goes on. It plays once a drive (after 10 minutes or more without the car), never
      before another item you tap in the car (Weak page, Next to learn, Manzil, a surah, Recite: it plays at once) or during a recitation on the phone; ⏭ skips it. Turn it off in Settings › Phone › Travel dua when the
      car starts.
                      • 
    
                    • Android Auto shows an app that does not come from Google Play only while its own Unknown sources setting
      is on: on the phone, Settings › Connected devices › Android Auto, scroll to the bottom and tap Version

      Unknown sources.
                      •

      re-plug); then ☰ › Send feedback right after the drive: it names Android Auto's last connection to
      Meddeb.
                      •

      (Settings › Apps › Android Auto › Storage › Clear cache; never Clear data, which also resets Unknown sources,

                      • 
    
                    • After a Meddeb update, Android Auto puts it at the end of a custom order: drag it back in Customize launcher,

                      •

      download.

      succeeded, every hour while none does).

                      •

                    Progress
                    
  
                      
    
                    • What to do now: three tasks (four with Review), old before new: Manzil, Weakest page,
      Review (where it exists) and Next to learn, each with a box. A green box with
       means done for today, an empty box means still to do. Built from everything you recited and marked; a word counts as a
      mistake when its latest reading is one.
                      • 
    
                    • Manzil: the page recited longest ago, to revise. Done while its age is green (recited less than 6 days
      ago); to do once it turns orange (6 days) or red (30 days). Once more than 10 pages hold an ayah you learned, you
      recite Al-Fatiha in every prayer: page 1 stays green and is never the Manzil.
                      • 
    
                    • Weakest page: the page with the most mistakes. Done unless its count is red (3 mistakes or more).
                      • 
    
                    • Next to learn: the first ayah with a word you never recited, moved back over the ayat with mistakes just
      before it. Done once it moved forward by at least one ayah today (since midnight on your device's clock); a new
      mistake can move it back. It stays to do while you never recite the ayah it names.
                      • 
    
                    • The table has one row per page holding an ayah you recited whole (every word read, mistakes or not).
      Err: its words counted as mistakes (green 0, orange 1-2, red 3+). Days ago: how long ago its recited
      part was last recited in full: today, 1 … 99 days, 99+ for longer (green under 6 days, orange from 6, red from 30;
      on a computer, hovering it shows the date).
                      • 
    
                    • Tap a column name to sort (again: reverse). Tap a page or a line to open it; an ayah link shows its ayah (it
      glows a moment), and the microphone follows you from anywhere on that page.

                    Review
                    
  
                      
    
                    • A word becomes a card when 2 of its last 5 readings were mistakes (a hint and + Mistake count too),
      counting only the readings after its ayah was learned; nearby words make one card. Progress has a line before Next to
      learn, Review: n due, done once no card is due today.
                      • 
    
                    • Each day the Review page lists up to 10 cards (3 new at most), the cards with the most mistakes first.
      To fix: mistakes made today, due again today; to
      confirm: read right once before today, and due today by the schedule.
                      • 
    
                    • Each card names its ayat (e.g. Al-Baqarah 12-13); two cards due the same day on ayat that follow each other
      are one card that day. Under the counters, a line says what today's mistakes became: in cards, or one more
      mistake to go.
                      • 
    
                    • Start opens the card's page in Blind with greyed lines before it (when the card starts at the top of a
      page, the page before, its last lines greyed): recite from where they end, on to the end of the card. A
      recitation still running is stopped and saved first. No timer.
                      • 
    
                    • A card starts with the microphone off: recite in your mind, ◂ / ⏮ show the next words as read (never a
      mistake in Blind or Shown; in Hidden they are hints); a word you got wrong: tap it, + Mistake. The microphone button recites aloud; the next cards then
      start with it on, until you switch it off with ■ (the card stays open).
                      • 
    
                    • The card's row sits just above the bar, its buttons always in the same places, from the left: ⋯ holds
      Suspend, Bury and Review list; ✕ leaves review mode (the page stays as it is, a recitation goes on); then
      ? and ▶ (with the result); ↻ starts the card again from its grey lines (as practice); at the
      right Skip (white: the next card; this one comes back at the end of today's round, skipped twice it waits
      until tomorrow), which turns into Finish (green) once you reach the end: press it to see the result, then
      Next card. Back (Esc) also goes back to the list.
                      • 
    
                    • The result: ✓ / ✗ for each ayah you recited, and when the card comes back: after a mistake, again today (at
      most 2 more times, each at least 45 minutes after the last: meanwhile it waits at the end of the list, "Again at
      10:45", and Next card takes the other cards), else when the schedule (FSRS) says. A card leaves for good once it passed its last 3 reviews
      on 3 different days and holds for 2 months; a later mistake brings it back.
                      • 
    
                    • With the result, ? says what went wrong (what was heard, the similar passages elsewhere in the Qur'an,
      the ones you learned first) and how to learn it, and ▶ plays the place as many times as Listen's Repeat says
      (17 = 18 plays), from the grey lines to the last mistake and 0-4 ayat more at random, with Listen's pause between
      the plays (▶ again stops it).
                      • 
    
                    • Suspend puts a card aside until you unsuspend it (the list at the bottom of the page); Bury waits
      until tomorrow. Any recitation counts for the cards: reciting their place in Recite reviews them too.

                    Stats
                    
  
                      
    
                    • Computed from the same history each time the page opens; nothing new is stored or sent. A day runs from
      midnight on your device's clock. An ayah is learned once you recited it at least once entirely without
      any mistake or hint (in one session; a false alarm you removed counts as right).
                      • 
    
                    • Today: the ayat you learned today; Mistakes corrected: words whose last reading before today was a
      mistake and that you recited right today, without a hint (a + Correct or a Remove alone, or a mistake made and
      fixed the same day, does not count); Pages recited: the words you recited today counted in pages (a
      whole page = 1, half a page = 0.5).
                      • 
    
                    • Current state: Total learned: learned ayat out of 6236. Perfect learning: learned ayat with no word counted as a
      mistake. Last learned: among the learned ayat with no mistake left, the one learned last (an ayah that
      still has a mistake is to learn again).
                      • 
    
                    • New ayat learned: the ayat you learned this week (from Monday) and this year. An ayah counts once, on
      the day of its first recitation without a mistake or hint; reciting it again does not count it again. Once the
      whole Qur'an is learned, a count at 0 is hidden.
                      • 
    
                    • Over time starts with Pace over: Since the beginning, the Last 4 weeks (the
      default) or the Last 7 days (the days since your first recitation while that is shorter, at least 7 days).
      Everything under it follows the choice, which is remembered.
                      • 
    
                    • New ayat a week and Pages recited a day: averages over those days (a day without a recitation
      counts 0).
                      • 
    
                    • The chart shows, day by day, Total learned, Perfect learning and Next to learn (its place in the mushaf in
      ayat: how many ayat come before it) over the days chosen in Pace over (from the day before them; its
      height starts near their lowest value), with each line's change a week over those days. Tap a name under it
      to hide or show its line; tap or point at the chart to read a day.
                      • 
    
                    • Finish date: when the whole Qur'an would be learned at the pace chosen in Pace over (the ayat
      left divided by the new ayat a week over those days). Choose a shorter window when the ayat you already knew,
      recited at first, make the pace too fast; the averages and the chart follow it too.
                      • 
    
                    • Mistake detection, over the same days: how well Meddeb finds your mistakes. Precision: of the
      mistakes Meddeb found, the share that were real (you did not remove them, by a double tap, Remove or + Correct).
      Recall: of your mistakes, the share Meddeb found (the ones it missed are those you added: + Mistake or a
      double tap). Words read in your mind (a card with the microphone off, Blind paused) and the words of a hint (◂)
      it had not found do not count. A mistake you never checked counts as real: the figures are only as good as your
      checks.
                      • 
    
                    • Hidden is practice: its correct words are not counted as recited (no pages, no ayat learned); its mistakes
      count.
