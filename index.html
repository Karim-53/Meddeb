Meddeb

A local and private Qur'an recitation coach

Meddeb listens while you recite from memory, follows you word by word on the Madani mushaf (Hafs an Asim) and marks the words you missed or changed. The speech recognition runs on your own phone or PC: your voice never leaves the device, and there is no account.

The automatic feedback can be wrong: it is help for your practice, not a ruling. Every verdict can be replayed from your own recording and corrected in one tap.

Status: the Android app is in private testing; the browser version will be published here.
